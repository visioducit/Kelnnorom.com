STEP 2.1: SIGN UP FOR ZOHO MAIL FOREVER FREE PLAN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Navigate to: https://www.zoho.com/mail/zohomail-pricing.html
2. Scroll to the very bottom of the page to the section titled "Forever Free Plan".
3. Click "Sign Up".
4. Choose "Sign up with a domain I already own".
5. Enter your domain: kelnnorom.com
6. Enter organization name (e.g. Kel Nnorom Advisory) and administrator details.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2.2: VERIFY DOMAIN OWNERSHIP IN GO54 DNS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. In the Zoho Mail setup wizard, select "TXT Method" for domain verification.
2. Zoho will display a unique verification record:
   - Host / Name:  @
   - TXT Value:    zoho-verification-code=zbXXXXXXXX.zmverify.zoho.com
3. Log in to GO54 (go54.com):
   - Go to "Domains" -> "Manage DNS" for kelnnorom.com.
   - Click "Add Record".
   - Type: TXT
   - Name: @
   - Value: [Paste your unique zoho-verification-code from Zoho]
   - TTL: 3600
4. Back in the Zoho setup wizard, click "Verify TXT Record". Zoho will verify
   ownership instantly.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2.3: CONFIGURE THE 3 ZOHO MX RECORDS IN GO54 DNS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
In GO54 DNS Zone Editor, delete any existing MX record pointing to
mail.kelnnorom.com and add these three MX records:

RECORD 1 (Primary MX):
  Type:       MX
  Host/Name:  @ (or kelnnorom.com)
  Target:     mx.zoho.com
  Priority:   10
  TTL:        3600

RECORD 2 (Secondary MX):
  Type:       MX
  Host/Name:  @ (or kelnnorom.com)
  Target:     mx2.zoho.com
  Priority:   20
  TTL:        3600

RECORD 3 (Tertiary MX):
  Type:       MX
  Host/Name:  @ (or kelnnorom.com)
  Target:     mx3.zoho.com
  Priority:   50
  TTL:        3600

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2.4: CONFIGURE SPF, DKIM, AND DMARC AUTHENTICATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A. SPF RECORD (TXT)
In GO54 DNS, add or edit the TXT record for @:
  Type:       TXT
  Host/Name:  @
  Value:      v=spf1 include:zoho.com ~all
  TTL:        3600

B. DKIM RECORD (Cryptographic Signing - TXT)
1. In Zoho Mail Admin Console, go to "Email Authentication" -> "DKIM".
2. Click "Add Selector". Name the selector: zoho
3. Zoho generates a 2048-bit RSA key.
4. In GO54 DNS, add a new TXT record:
  Type:       TXT
  Host/Name:  zoho._domainkey
  Value:      v=DKIM1; k=rsa; p=MIGfMA0GCSqGSIb3DQEBAQUAA4GNADCBiQ... [from Zoho]
  TTL:        3600
5. In Zoho Admin Console, click "Verify" and toggle status to "Active".

C. DMARC POLICY RECORD (Anti-Spoofing - TXT)
In GO54 DNS, add:
  Type:       TXT
  Host/Name:  _dmarc
  Value:      v=DMARC1; p=quarantine; rua=mailto:dmarc@kelnnorom.com; pct=100; aspf=r;
  TTL:        3600

D. CUSTOM WEBMAIL LOGIN URL (Optional CNAME)
To allow team members to access webmail via https://webmail.kelnnorom.com:
  Type:       CNAME
  Host/Name:  webmail
  Value:      business.zoho.com
  TTL:        3600

7. TROUBLESHOOTING COMMON MAIL ISSUES
================================================================================

Issue: SMTP connection timeout on port 465
Fix:   If using cPanel, confirm the "mail" A record points to your cPanel IP,
       NOT Vercel. If using Zoho, ensure outbound SMTP host is smtppro.zoho.com.

Issue: Emails bouncing with "550 No Such User Here" or "Relay Access Denied"
Fix:   For cPanel: Check cPanel -> Email Routing -> Set "Local Mail Exchanger".
       For Zoho: Confirm MX records in GO54 point to mx.zoho.com, NOT cPanel.

Issue: Emails landing in Gmail Spam / Junk folder
Fix:   Ensure SPF TXT record includes "include:zoho.com" and DKIM key is
       published and verified in Zoho Admin Console.
